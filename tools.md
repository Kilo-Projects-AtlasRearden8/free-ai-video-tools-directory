## How to use a listing

Each tool below includes beginner steps. Pick one tool, follow the steps, and publish your first video.

### DaVinci Resolve
1. Download the free version from the official link and install it.
2. Open DaVinci Resolve, create a new project, and import your video clips into the Media Pool.
3. Drag clips to the timeline on the Edit page, trim, add transitions and titles.
4. Use the Color page for AI-powered color matching and correction.
5. Deliver (export) your video in MP4 format.

### CapCut
1. Install CapCut (desktop or mobile) or open the web editor.
2. Import your video and drag it onto the timeline.
3. Use Auto Cut to let AI assemble highlights, or add Auto Captions on the Text tab.
4. Apply effects, transitions, and music from the free library.
5. Export and download your video.

### Clipchamp
1. Open clipchamp.com in your browser or open the Clipchamp app on Windows/macOS.
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account, create a video, and import your media.
3. Use AI features such as auto compose, text-to-speech, and speaker coach.
4. Edit on the timeline, add filters and transitions.
5. Export in 1080p (free tier) and download.

### Canva
1. Go to canva.com and search "video" to pick a template or start blank.
2. Upload your clips or use Canva's free stock video library.
3. Use Magic Edit and AI tools to adjust clips, add text, animations, and music.
4. Download as MP4 (free tier limits apply to some premium assets).

### OpusClip
1. Go to opusclip.com and paste a YouTube/video URL or upload a file.
2. Choose captions style and clip length preferences.
3. Let AI generate short clips with captions from the long video.
4. Review, edit the transcript-based timeline, and download.

### Whisper
1. Install Python on your computer if you don't have it.
2. Run: `pip install openai-whisper` and `pip install ffmpeg-python` (ffmpeg must be installed).
3. Run: `whisper your-video.mp4 --model base`
4. Whisper generates subtitle files (SRT, VTT, TXT) in the current folder.

### VEED.io
1. Go to veed.io and upload your video to the online editor.
2. Choose Auto Subtitles from the Subtitles tab to generate captions.
3. Edit the caption text and timing, then style fonts and colors.
4. Export (free tier includes a watermark) and download.

### Kapwing
1. Go to kapwing.com and start a new project with your video.
2. Open the Subtitles tool and choose Auto-generate.
3. Review the AI transcript, fix any errors, and set the style.
4. Export and download (free tier is watermarked).

### Submagic
1. Go to submagic.co and upload your video.
2. Select the language and caption template.
3. AI generates dynamic captions with emojis and keywords highlighted.
4. Download the captioned video (free tier limits monthly exports).

### Adobe Express
1. Go to adobe.com/express and open the thumbnail maker.
2. Pick a YouTube thumbnail template or start from scratch.
3. Upload your video frame or image, add AI-generated text effects and graphics.
4. Download as PNG or JPG.

### Fotor
1. Go to fotor.com and open the YouTube thumbnail creator.
2. Choose a template, upload your image, and use AI enhancement tools.
3. Add text, stickers, and effects.
4. Download your thumbnail.

### Simplified
1. Go to simplified.com and open the AI thumbnail generator.
2. Describe your video topic and choose a style.
3. Generate thumbnail options and edit text, colors, and images.
4. Download the design.

### Media.io
1. Go to media.io and open the online video enhancer.
2. Upload your video and choose enhancement options (upscale, denoise, color correct).
3. Process the video in the browser.
4. Download the enhanced result (free tier is watermarked).
